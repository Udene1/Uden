# Commercial Opportunities

An opportunity exists when evidence supports a plausible business problem and a reachable buyer.

Record:
- company
- evidence
- problem
- buyer
- possible solution(s)
- why the solution is credible
- proposed offer
- conversation status
- commercial value if known
- next action

The solution can be any credible sellable capability. Do not force Cashflow OS, Compflow, or engineering into an opportunity without evidence.
