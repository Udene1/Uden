# Cognitia — Decisions

- Keep Cognitia private while its capabilities are being understood.
- Do not use LLM-generated explanations as evidence of cognitive capability.
- Prefer deterministic experiments with inspectable artifacts.
- Reduce hand-holding progressively.
- For each experiment: build → inspect CI artifacts → record research result → merge before moving to the next experiment.
- Treat experience as defeasible evidence, not instruction.
