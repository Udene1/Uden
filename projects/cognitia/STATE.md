# Cognitia — State

Cognitia is a private research project exploring a general artificial cognitive system rather than a conventional LLM wrapper or benchmark optimizer.

Research areas include world/state representation, evidence and provenance, hypotheses, causal/counterfactual reasoning, uncertainty and contradiction, consequences, intellectual experience, abstraction transfer, and communicative objectives that preserve epistemic truth.

Current research method: zero hand-holding. Experiments should reveal actual capability boundaries.

Recent work has demonstrated that retrieved experience can affect candidate evaluations without necessarily changing an action boundary. A source audit also established that an Experience.state_update field was not itself evidence of an active state transition.

A current line of investigation is whether the codebase is being ingested as experience and whether underlying structure can transfer across representations/languages, including code used across mathematical and economic problems.
